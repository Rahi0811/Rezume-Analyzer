How it Works

From upload to insight
in three steps.

No configuration. No training data. Just paste a JD, drop your resumes, and get ranked results powered by frontier AI.

01
Define the Role
Paste your job description — requirements, responsibilities, tech stack, experience level. The richer, the better.
02
Upload Resumes
Drag and drop any number of PDF resumes. NeuralHire extracts full text using an intelligent parsing layer.
03
Get Ranked Results
Gemini 2.5 Flash evaluates each candidate semantically — returning match scores, strengths, gaps, and hire verdicts.

Capabilities

Everything your hiring team
actually needs.

Built on the Gemini API with structured JSON output — so your data is always clean, always actionable.

Smart PDF Parsing
Advanced PyPDF2 extraction handles multi-page resumes, complex layouts, and embedded text with high fidelity.
Semantic Alignment
Goes beyond keyword matching. Gemini understands context, synonyms, and implied skills across your entire candidate pool.
Score & Ranking
Every candidate receives a 0–100 match score. Your shortlist is auto-sorted — the best fits rise to the top instantly.
Gap Analysis
Identifies precisely what each candidate is missing — whether it's certifications, domain experience, or technical depth.
Hire Verdict Engine
AI-generated verdicts — Shortlist, Backup, or Reject — give your team clear, defensible hiring signals at a glance.
CSV Export
Download your complete screening report as a structured CSV — ready for ATS import, team review, or compliance archiving.
